Flagship project

Dividend- and rights-adjusted NEPSE price series

This project constructs adjusted OHLC series from raw NEPSE prices so that charts and models are not distorted by ex-dividend gaps, rights issues, or splits.

1. Data extraction

Historical stock prices

More than twelve years of history were collected in Python (Beautiful Soup) from the official NEPSE site. Example scraping workflow: Today’s Price Download notebook.

Dividend history

Dividend announcements were sourced from ShareSansar and structured for adjustment. Reference notebook: Dividend history from ShareSansar.

Intermediate tables were versioned as CSV for traceability and downstream use.

2. Data cleaning

Source data contained missing values, outliers, and structural inconsistencies. Visual checks and validation notebooks were used before and after adjustment. Cleaning and sanity checks: Data Cleaning.

3. Adjustment methodology

After extraction and cleaning, a backward-adjusted algorithm was applied so that historical levels reflect dividends, rights, and splits consistently. The chart below compares unadjusted versus adjusted closes for Nepal Life Insurance Company Ltd. (NLIC): gaps on the ex-dates in the raw series are reconciled in the adjusted panel.

Visualization

News headline word clouds (Tableau)

Roughly seven years of headlines from MyRepublica were collected with Python (Beautiful Soup), then explored in a Tableau dashboard. Users can filter by year, news category, and frequency band to inspect term usage across about 84,000 titles and 800,000 tokens.

Technical strategy backtesting

A custom backtesting engine was implemented to evaluate a rules-based strategy using the adjusted price series above as input.

  • Buy when price is above the 20-day moving average, RSI is above 50, and the MACD line is above the signal line.
  • Sell when price is below the 20-day moving average, RSI is below 50, and the MACD line is below the signal line.
