Dividend- and rights-adjusted NEPSE price series

Adjusted OHLC from raw NEPSE prices so charts and models are not distorted by ex-dividend gaps, rights issues, or splits.

1. Data extraction

Historical stock prices

More than twelve years of history were collected in Python (Beautiful Soup) from the official NEPSE site. Example workflow: Today’s Price Download notebook.

Dividend history

Dividend announcements from ShareSansar, structured for adjustment. Reference: Dividend history from ShareSansar. Tables were kept as CSV for traceability.

2. Data cleaning

Missing values, outliers, and inconsistencies were flagged with visual checks and validation notebooks. Data Cleaning.

3. Adjustment methodology

A backward-adjusted algorithm aligns historical levels with dividends, rights, and splits. Below: NLIC unadjusted versus adjusted closes — ex-date gaps in the raw series are reconciled in the adjusted panel.
